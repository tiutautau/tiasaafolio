Lakipalvelu Minna Ikonen

❮
❯

Website illustrations for a legal services company for (mainly) forest owners. The client wanted a playful, approachable style rather than a traditional corporate look, with clear and minimal typography.

I collaborated closely with a web developer to design the overall website experience. While the developer handled layout and technical implementation, I was responsible for the visual concept and all illustrations. We worked together exchanging ideas and refining the design together to ensure cohesion between visuals and structure.

I designed a colorful yet calm forest landscape for the homepage and Finnish forest-inspired elements for other pages, using Procreate and Illustrator.

"Suomuurain"

❮
❯

School project where I designed a pattern for imaginery brand "Finlaysön". Logo is not part of the pattern and it is modified Finlayson's logo. It is just for presentation purposes. Pattern's name is "Suomuurain", which is one of the many names for cloudberry in Finnish. I approached the cloudberry design with Finlayson's aesthetic and product range in mind.

The design of the pattern was shaped by my memories of late-summer days spent on the cloudberry bog with my grandparents. The pattern was then used in an ad to showcase how it could be used in social media or digital outdoors marketing materials.

This assignment was actually one of my entrance exam assignments for Metropolia. The idea was to create a poster for "Helsinki 2036" for when Helsinki would presumably host the Olympic Games.
