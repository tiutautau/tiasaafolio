Täydennä tarinaa

❮ ❯

Educational storytelling game developed with Kehitysvammaliitto and Metropolia to promote language learning through play. The game introduces players to narrative-building mechanics that adapt to user choices. Priority was given to accessibility and inclusivity in both design and content.

In this project, I worked with a team to come up with the layout design, overall style and game assets. I was responsible for music design and I also made many of the game assets.
