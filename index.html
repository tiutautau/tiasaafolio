Lakipalvelu Minna Ikonen

❮
❯

Website illustration for a client for her new company which offers legal services mainly for forest owners. Client wished for a playful forest illustration and didn't want any "serious corporation vibe". Typography clear and minimalistic.

I worked as a team with a Web Developer to design the whole website. I made the illustrations using Procreate and Illustrator and he made the layout and all of the programming. We communicated a lot together about ideas and design.

I designed a forest landscape picture for the home page and some finnish forest elements for the other pages. I wanted to make something colourful but still keep some calmness in all of the illustrations.

Suomuurain

❮
❯

School project where I designed a pattern for imaginery brand "Finlaysön". Logo is not part of the pattern and it is modified Finlayson's logo. It is just for presentation purposes. Pattern's name is "Suomuurain", which is one of the many names for cloudberry in Finnish. I approached the cloudberry design with Finlayson's aesthetic and product range in mind.

The design of the pattern was shaped by my memories of late-summer days spent on the cloudberry bog with my grandparents. The pattern was then used in an ad to showcase how it could be used in social media or digital outdoors marketing materials.
